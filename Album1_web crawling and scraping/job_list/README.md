# Job Information Scraping

## Objective
Scrape job postings for **“Crawler Engineer”** from the Shixiseng website, then analyze and visualize the distribution of cities, salaries, and job counts. Use bar charts to present the average salary and number of positions across different cities.

## Process
1. Define a function with a URL parameter to batch process each page.  
2. Scrape details from individual job pages and save the data.  
3. Read and process the data (calculate average salary and job counts).  
4. Plot bar charts to visualize the results.  
5. Scrape multiple job listing pages, iterate through each job, and call the function.  

- For practice, only the first two pages of job postings are scraped.  
- The dataset of job postings, bar charts of average salary and job counts by city, and a demo video are provided as references.
